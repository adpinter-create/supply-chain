About this project

This planner is a browser version of a monthly demand and supply planning workbook: sales history, open orders, account forecasts, inventory and inbound supply in one place, with a planner setting the forecast for every SKU in every warehouse.

  • SKU planner. Each SKU shows 18 months of units sold, booked orders, account forecasts and the plan. Drag the plan points or type values; quick buttons set the plan from recent averages, last year or the account forecast.
  • Projected inventory. Stock on hand plus inbound supply, minus the larger of the plan or booked orders each month. The band shows the target cover you set.
  • Exceptions. Rules flag projected shortages, excess stock, plans far from the run rate and demand with no plan.
  • Views. Show units or revenue, and filter by warehouse, category and brand.

Every product, brand, account, warehouse figure, price and date here is invented. Edits are saved in your browser only.
